{#- **One hero, and the page is the animation.** The identity used to be told three times over — the bar, this block, and an eye of its own inside a dark panel below the story — which is three claims on the same attention and no hierarchy at all. So the gaze *is* the stage now: the terms she watches orbit behind these eyes, on the page's own canvas, at the prose's own width, and the animation **settles** before the story starts rather than running under it. Colour carries one meaning each: **green is seen**, and it is spent only on the term passing the eyes and on the phrase in the paragraph that term names — which is what makes *my eyes stay on the things you care about* literal rather than decorative. **Pink is her**, and nothing else takes it. Everything at rest is muted. Monospace is for the **signals** only: a term is a machine's word, a sentence is hers, and the two should not be set in one face.
