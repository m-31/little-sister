{# What the log leaves out (ADR-0121 decision 7). Always shown, like the policy above: there is always a log, and a log that leaves lines out has to say which somewhere other than in itself — a deployment that takes the library's own lines out with `LOG_LEVEL` has this page for it. A cap is declared by the package that brings the noise and set by the library, so neither the deployment's code nor its log shows that it happened; and whether a cap *holds* is decided against what the deployment said, in its code or in the variable. The page lays the three side by side: each logger that carries a level of its own, with where the level comes from. Read off the loggers as they stand at the request, not as startup left them — what code sets after the import, it sets, and a page that still called that level a cap would be wrong in exactly the case somebody opens it for. #}
